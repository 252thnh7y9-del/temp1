Gymnastics

Strength and grace, judged to a fraction of a point.

Competitors
Individual, plus team competitions
Women's apparatus
Vault, uneven bars, balance beam, floor
Men's apparatus
Floor, pommel horse, rings, vault, parallel bars, high bar
Biggest events
The Olympics, the World Championships and NCAA championships

What is it?

Artistic gymnastics is the Olympic headliner. Gymnasts perform routines on different pieces of equipment (apparatus), combining flips, twists, holds and balance. A panel of judges scores each routine for difficulty and how cleanly it's performed.

It's one of the most-watched events at every Summer Olympics. In the US it's also a big college sport, where NCAA women's meets draw sold-out crowds.

The basic rules

  1. Each routine gets a difficulty score (how hard the skills are) plus an execution score out of 10 (how cleanly they're done).
  2. Judges deduct for bent knees, wobbles, steps on landing and falls. A fall costs a full point.
  3. Elite gymnastics dropped the “perfect 10” in 2006, so top scores now look like 14 or 15. College gymnastics still uses the perfect-10 system.
  4. The all-around title goes to the best combined score across every apparatus.
  5. Skills are named after the first gymnast to perform them in major competition. That's why several are called “the Biles.”

Famous teams

  • USA womenThe dominant team of the modern era.
  • Soviet Union (historic)Ruled the sport for decades.
  • ChinaA powerhouse, especially on bars and beam.
  • Japan menOlympic team champions in 2004, 2016 and 2024.
  • Oklahoma Sooners / LSU / UCLACollege powerhouses.

Famous names to know

  • Simone BilesAmerican with the most world and Olympic medals of any gymnast. The GOAT.
  • Nadia ComăneciRomanian who scored the first-ever perfect 10 at the 1976 Olympics, aged 14.
  • Kōhei UchimuraJapanese “King Kohei,” two-time Olympic all-around champion.
  • Olga KorbutSoviet star who popularized the sport in 1972.
  • Suni LeeAmerican, the 2020 Olympic all-around champion.
  • Katelyn OhashiHer joyful UCLA floor routine went viral.

Lingo you'll hear

Stick the landing
Land without moving your feet.
Dismount
The final skill to leave an apparatus.
Salute
Raising arms to the judges before and after a routine.
Twisties
A dangerous loss of air awareness mid-twist.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“That's a big deduction for the step on landing.” Commentators say it all the time.
