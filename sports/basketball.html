Basketball

Fast, high-scoring, and played above the rim.

Players on the court
5 per team
Game length
NBA: 4 quarters × 12 min (about 2.5 hours real time)
Season
October to June
Big final
The NBA Finals, and “March Madness” in college

What is it?

Basketball is played by two teams of five on an indoor court. Each team tries to shoot the ball through a hoop 10 feet off the ground. Scoring is constant, often more than 100 points per team in the NBA, so the action rarely stops.

Invented in 1891 by James Naismith in Massachusetts, it is now one of the most global sports, and its stars are some of the most famous athletes on the planet.

The basic rules

  1. A basket counts 2 points, or 3 points if shot from behind the curved three-point line.
  2. Free throws, unguarded shots after certain fouls, are worth 1 point each.
  3. You must dribble (bounce) the ball while moving. Walking with it is traveling.
  4. The shot clock gives a team 24 seconds (NBA) to shoot, so nobody can stall.
  5. Too much physical contact is a foul. In the NBA, a player with 6 fouls must leave the game.
  6. Ties go to 5-minute overtime periods until someone wins.

Major leagues & competitions

  • NBANational Basketball Association. 30 teams in the US and Canada.
  • WNBAThe top women's professional league, growing fast.
  • NCAACollege basketball. Its March Madness tournament is famous for upsets and office bracket pools.
  • EuroLeagueThe top club competition in Europe.

Famous teams

  • Los Angeles LakersHollywood's team: Magic, Kareem, Kobe, Shaq, LeBron.
  • Boston CelticsTied with the Lakers for the most NBA titles.
  • Chicago BullsMichael Jordan's six-title dynasty of the 1990s.
  • Golden State WarriorsRevolutionized the game with three-point shooting in the 2010s.
  • Harlem GlobetrottersAn exhibition team famous for comedy and trick shots.

Famous names to know

  • Michael JordanSix titles with the Bulls, the most common pick for greatest ever.
  • LeBron JamesFour titles and the NBA's all-time leading scorer.
  • Stephen CurryThe greatest shooter ever, who made the three-pointer king.
  • Kobe BryantFive titles with the Lakers. Known for his “Mamba mentality.”
  • Caitlin ClarkRecord-breaking college scorer turned WNBA star.
  • Shaquille O'NealGiant center, now a beloved TV personality.

Lingo you'll hear

Dunk
Slamming the ball down through the hoop.
Triple-double
Reaching 10+ in three stats (like points, rebounds, assists) in one game.
Rebound
Grabbing a missed shot.
Buzzer-beater
A shot that goes in as time expires.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“Jordan or LeBron?” This debate never ends, and both answers are defensible.
