Figure Skating

Athletic jumps and artistry on ice, judged to music.

Competitors
Singles, pairs, ice dance and a team event
Programs
Short program (~3 min) + free skate (~4 min)
Season
October to March
Biggest events
The Olympics and the World Championships

What is it?

Figure skaters perform routines to music, combining spinning jumps, spins and footwork with artistry and expression. Judges score both the difficulty and quality of the elements and the overall performance.

It's one of the most-watched Winter Olympic sports, and its stars become household names. Modern skaters land quadruple jumps, rotating four times in under a second.

The basic rules

  1. Each skater performs a short program and a free skate. The scores are added together.
  2. Each jump, spin and step sequence has a base value based on difficulty, plus a quality score from the judges.
  3. Judges also give program component scores for skating skills, composition and presentation.
  4. Falls cost a deduction, and under-rotated jumps lose value.
  5. Ice dance has no big jumps. It focuses on rhythm, footwork and lifts.

Major leagues & competitions

  • Winter OlympicsFive events.
  • World Figure Skating ChampionshipsEvery March.
  • Grand Prix seriesSix autumn events and a final.
  • European / Four Continents ChampionshipsRegional championships.

Powerhouse nations

  • United StatesA long tradition of stars.
  • JapanHugely popular there, with modern legends.
  • CanadaStrong in pairs and ice dance.
  • RussiaHistorically dominant, currently banned from international events.

Famous names to know

  • Yuzuru HanyuJapanese two-time Olympic champion (2014, 2018) with a massive fan base.
  • Ilia MalininAmerican “Quad God,” the first to land a quadruple axel in competition.
  • Michelle KwanAmerican five-time world champion.
  • Katarina WittEast German two-time Olympic champion.
  • Torvill & DeanBritish ice dancers whose 1984 “Boléro” earned perfect scores.

Lingo you'll hear

Axel
The only jump taken off going forward, making it the hardest.
Quad
A jump with four rotations.
Kiss and cry
Where skaters wait for their scores.
Triple axel
Three and a half rotations.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“Was that jump fully rotated?” Under-rotation is the commentators' favorite topic.
