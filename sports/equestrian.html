What is it?

Equestrian sport tests the partnership between horse and rider. It's one of the few Olympic sports where men and women compete against each other on equal terms.

There are three disciplines. Dressage is precise, ballet-like movements. Show jumping means clearing a course of fences. Eventing combines both with a demanding cross-country ride.

The basic rules

  1. Dressage: judges score each movement in a set test.
  2. Show jumping: knocking down a rail costs 4 penalty points, and a horse refusing a jump also costs points. Fewest penalties wins.
  3. Ties at zero go to a jump-off against the clock.
  4. Eventing: a dressage score, plus cross-country penalties, plus show jumping penalties.
  5. Horses get veterinary checks throughout.
