Cycling (Tour de France)

Three weeks, 3,500 km, mountains and a yellow jersey.

Teams
About 22 teams of 8 riders in the Tour
Race length
Tour de France: 21 stages, about 3,500 km
Season
February to October
Biggest events
The three Grand Tours and the one-day “Monuments”

What is it?

Professional road cycling is a team sport disguised as an individual one. Teams of riders race across hundreds of kilometers a day. Most of the team works to protect and shelter one leader, who aims to win.

Its crown jewel is the Tour de France: 21 stages over three weeks every July, finishing in Paris. Millions line the roads for free, and TV coverage doubles as a tour of the French countryside.

The basic rules

  1. In stage races, each rider's times are added up. The lowest total time wears the leader's jersey, yellow in the Tour.
  2. Other jerseys: green for points (sprinters), polka dot for the best climber and white for the best young rider.
  3. Riding behind others (drafting) saves about 30% of your energy, which is why riders travel in a big pack, the peloton.
  4. Helper riders, called domestiques, fetch water, block wind and chase down attacks for their leader.
  5. On flat stages, sprinters usually win. Mountain stages and time trials decide the overall winner.

Major leagues & competitions

  • Tour de FranceJuly. The biggest race in the world.
  • Giro d'ItaliaItaly's Grand Tour in May. The leader wears pink.
  • Vuelta a EspañaSpain's Grand Tour in late summer. The leader wears red.
  • The MonumentsFive classic one-day races, including the cobbled Paris–Roubaix.
  • Tour de France FemmesThe women's Tour, relaunched in 2022.

Famous teams

  • UAE Team EmiratesTadej Pogačar's team, the dominant force of the 2020s.
  • Visma–Lease a BikeDutch team of Jonas Vingegaard.
  • INEOS GrenadiersFormerly Team Sky, which won seven Tours in eight years (2012–2019).
  • Soudal Quick-StepBelgian team famous for one-day classics.

Famous names to know

  • Eddy MerckxBelgian “Cannibal” who won everything, including 5 Tours. Widely seen as the greatest.
  • Tadej PogačarSlovenian superstar, a multiple Tour winner of the 2020s.
  • Jonas VingegaardDanish two-time Tour winner and Pogačar's great rival.
  • Miguel InduráinSpaniard who won five straight Tours in the 1990s.
  • Marianne VosDutch legend of women's cycling.

Lingo you'll hear

Peloton
The main pack of riders.
Breakaway
A small group that escapes ahead of the peloton.
Domestique
A helper rider.
Bonk
Running completely out of energy.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“The Tour is won in the mountains.” Almost always true.
