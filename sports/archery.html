Archery

Hit a target 70 meters away, under pressure, with a bow.

Competitors
Individual, team and mixed team
Distance
70 meters
Target
122 cm wide, 10 scoring rings
Biggest events
The Olympics and the World Championships

What is it?

Olympic archers shoot arrows at a target 70 meters (77 yards) away, with a gold center about the size of a CD. They use a recurve bow, with no magnifying sights, and must stay calm while the wind blows and the crowd watches.

Matches are head-to-head duels. South Korea is the sport's superpower: its women's team has won every Olympic team gold since 1988.

The basic rules

  1. The target's rings score from 1 (outer) to 10 (the gold center).
  2. Matches are played in sets of 3 arrows each. Win a set for 2 points, tie it for 1.
  3. The first archer to 6 set points wins.
  4. A tied match goes to a shoot-off: one arrow each, and the closest to the center wins.
  5. Each arrow must be shot within a time limit.

Lingo you'll hear

X
The innermost ring of the 10, used for tiebreaks.
Recurve
The bow used at the Olympics.
Compound
A bow with pulleys, used in other events.
Shoot-off
A one-arrow tiebreaker.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“Korea's archery selection is tougher than the Olympics.” Making their team is notoriously hard.
