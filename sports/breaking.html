Breaking

Breakdancing battles: hip-hop culture on the Olympic stage.

Competitors
1 vs 1 battles
Format
Alternating rounds of about a minute
Music
Chosen live by the DJ
Olympic events
B-boys and B-girls (Paris 2024)

What is it?

Breaking (breakdancing) was born in the Bronx in the 1970s as part of hip-hop culture. B-boys and B-girls battle one-on-one, improvising to music the DJ chooses, with footwork, spins, power moves and freezes.

It made its Olympic debut at Paris 2024 but isn't on the program for LA 2028.

The basic rules

  1. Two dancers take turns performing short sets, called rounds.
  2. Judges score technique, vocabulary (variety of moves), execution, musicality and originality.
  3. Each judge votes for the winner of each round. The most rounds wins.
  4. The DJ picks the music, so dancers must improvise.
  5. Biting (copying an opponent's move) is frowned upon.

  • B-Boy Phil Wizard (Philip Kim)Canadian, the first Olympic men's breaking champion.
  • B-Girl RaygunAustralian whose unusual Olympic performance went viral.

Lingo you'll hear

Toprock
Standing footwork.
Downrock
Footwork on the floor.
Power move
Spinning moves like windmills and headspins.
Freeze
Holding a pose, often upside down.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“That freeze was right on the beat.” Musicality is a big part of scoring.
