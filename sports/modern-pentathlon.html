What is it?

Modern pentathlon was created by Pierre de Coubertin, founder of the modern Olympics. It was meant to test the skills of a 19th-century cavalry officer behind enemy lines: fencing, swimming, riding, running and shooting.

Riding horses was replaced by an obstacle course (like a TV ninja course) from LA 2028 onwards.

The basic rules

  1. Fencing: quick épée bouts against everyone, plus a bonus round.
  2. Swimming: 200 m freestyle.
  3. Riding (until 2024): show jumping on an unfamiliar horse. Obstacle course from 2028.
  4. Points from each event decide the starting order for the final laser-run: running plus laser-pistol shooting.
  5. The first across the finish line in the laser-run wins.
