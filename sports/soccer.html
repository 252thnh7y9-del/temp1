Soccer (Football)

The world's game: one ball, two goals, no hands.

Players on the field
11 per team, including a goalkeeper
Game length
2 halves × 45 min, plus “stoppage time”
Season
August to May in Europe, February to December in MLS
Big final
The World Cup (every 4 years) and the Champions League final (every year)

What is it?

Soccer, called “football” almost everywhere outside the US, is the most popular sport on Earth. Two teams of 11 try to kick or head the ball into the other team's net. Only the goalkeepers may use their hands.

Goals are rare (a 1–0 game is normal), so each one is a huge moment. The FIFA World Cup, held every four years, is the most-watched sporting event in the world. The 2026 edition is hosted by the USA, Canada and Mexico.

The basic rules

  1. Score by getting the whole ball over the goal line between the posts.
  2. No hands or arms, except for the goalkeeper inside their own penalty area.
  3. Offside: an attacker can't be closer to the goal than the second-to-last defender at the moment a teammate passes to them. Basically, no lurking by the goal.
  4. Fouls give the other team a free kick. A foul inside the penalty area gives a penalty kick: one shooter against the goalie from 12 yards.
  5. A yellow card is a warning. A second yellow or a straight red card sends the player off, and the team plays a player short.
  6. League games can end in a tie (a “draw”). Knockout games go to extra time and then a penalty shootout.

Major leagues & competitions

  • English Premier LeagueThe richest and most-watched league in the world.
  • La Liga (Spain)Home of Real Madrid and Barcelona.
  • Serie A (Italy), Bundesliga (Germany), Ligue 1 (France)The rest of Europe's “Big Five” leagues.
  • UEFA Champions LeagueA yearly tournament between the best club teams in Europe.
  • MLSMajor League Soccer, the top league in the US and Canada.
  • FIFA World CupNational teams, every four years.

Famous teams

  • Real MadridThe record winner of the European Cup / Champions League.
  • FC BarcelonaKnown for its passing style and for being Messi's longtime club.
  • Manchester UnitedEngland's most famous club around the world.
  • LiverpoolFamous for its anthem, “You'll Never Walk Alone.”
  • Bayern MunichGermany's powerhouse, winning the league almost every year.
  • Brazil (national team)Five World Cup titles, more than any other country.

Famous names to know

  • Lionel MessiArgentine magician who won the 2022 World Cup. Often called the GOAT.
  • Cristiano RonaldoPortuguese superstar, all-time leading international goal scorer.
  • PeléBrazilian legend who won three World Cups.
  • Diego MaradonaArgentine icon, famous for the “Hand of God” goal in 1986.
  • Kylian MbappéFrench forward, a World Cup winner at 19.
  • Erling HaalandNorwegian goal-scoring machine.

Lingo you'll hear

Pitch
The field.
Clean sheet
When a team doesn't concede a goal.
Nil
Zero, as in “two-nil.”
Hat trick
One player scoring three goals in a game.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“VAR is ruining the game.” The video review system is the most reliable complaint in soccer.
