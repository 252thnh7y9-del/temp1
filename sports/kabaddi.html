Kabaddi

Tag meets wrestling, from India. Raid, touch and escape.

Players
7 per team on court
Game length
2 halves × 20 min
Raid time
30 seconds per raid in the pro league
Biggest events
Pro Kabaddi League, Asian Games, Kabaddi World Cup

What is it?

Kabaddi is a contact sport from ancient India. One player, the raider, enters the opponent's half, tags as many defenders as possible and runs back, all without being tackled. Traditionally, raiders had to do it in a single breath, chanting “kabaddi, kabaddi” to prove it.

The Pro Kabaddi League, launched in 2014, turned it into one of India's most-watched sports. It's also played across South Asia and in Iran.

The basic rules

  1. Teams take turns sending one raider into the other half.
  2. The raider scores 1 point for each defender tagged, if they make it back to their own half.
  3. If defenders tackle the raider and stop them returning, the defense scores 1 point.
  4. Tagged or tackled players are out, but come back in when their team scores.
  5. Getting the whole opposing team out (an all out) earns 2 bonus points.
  6. Tackling the raider when your team has 3 or fewer players left is a super tackle, worth 2 points.

Major leagues & competitions

  • Pro Kabaddi League (PKL)India's glitzy professional league.
  • Asian GamesKabaddi has been a medal sport since 1990.
  • Kabaddi World CupContested by national teams.

Famous teams

  • IndiaLong dominant at the Asian Games.
  • IranIndia's fiercest rival, and 2018 Asian Games men's champion.
  • Patna PiratesThree-time PKL champions.
  • Jaipur Pink PanthersWon the first PKL title.

Famous names to know

  • Pardeep Narwal“Dubki King,” famous for ducking under defenders. The PKL's record raid scorer.
  • Anup KumarIndia's “Captain Cool.”
  • Pawan SehrawatA high-flying raider known for leaping over tackles.
  • Fazel AtrachaliIranian defender and PKL legend.

Lingo you'll hear

Raid
One attack into the opponent's half.
Do-or-die raid
A raid where the raider must score or be out.
Bonus line
Crossing it with a foot earns an extra point.
Ankle hold
The classic defensive tackle.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“Strong defense wins PKL titles.” Tackle points often decide close matches.
