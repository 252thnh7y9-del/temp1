One of the oldest sports: takedowns, holds and pins.

What is it?

Wrestling dates back to the ancient Olympics. Two athletes grapple to take each other down and pin their opponent's shoulders to the mat. Olympic wrestling has two styles: freestyle, where legs can be attacked, and Greco-Roman, where holds below the waist are banned.

It's hugely popular in Iran, Russia, Turkey, Japan and the United States, where high school and college wrestling are big.

The basic rules

  1. A pin (fall), holding both shoulders to the mat, wins instantly.
  2. A takedown (bringing your opponent to the mat with control) scores points.
  3. Exposing the opponent's back to the mat also scores.
  4. Leading by 10 points (8 in Greco-Roman) wins by technical superiority.
  5. Passivity is penalized.
