Volleyball

Three touches, over the net, never let it drop.

Players
6 per team indoors, 2 on the beach
Match length
Best of 5 sets, about 1.5–2 hours
Season
Varies by league. US college volleyball is in the fall.
Biggest events
The Olympics, the World Championships and the Volleyball Nations League

What is it?

Volleyball is played by two teams of six separated by a high net. Each team tries to ground the ball on the other side, using up to three touches to pass, set and spike it over. It's fast, acrobatic and one of the most widely played sports in the world.

There's also beach volleyball, played two-a-side on sand. Both are Olympic sports, and US college women's volleyball draws enormous crowds.

The basic rules

  1. Each team gets up to 3 touches to return the ball. Usually that's pass (dig), set, then attack (spike).
  2. A player can't touch the ball twice in a row (a touch while blocking doesn't count).
  3. Every rally scores a point, no matter who served.
  4. Sets go to 25 points, and you must win by 2. A deciding 5th set goes to 15.
  5. After winning the serve back, players rotate positions clockwise.
  6. The libero, in a different-colored shirt, is a defensive specialist who can't attack above the net.

Famous teams

  • ItalyMen won world titles in 2022 and 2025, and the women took 2024 Olympic gold.
  • PolandFanatical support and a perennial contender.
  • USA womenWon Olympic gold in 2020.
  • Nebraska CornhuskersA college volleyball dynasty with huge crowds.

Famous names to know

  • Karch KiralyAmerican with Olympic golds in both indoor and beach volleyball.
  • Kerri Walsh Jennings & Misty May-TreanorWon three straight Olympic beach golds (2004–2012).
  • GibaBrazilian icon of the 2000s.
  • Zhu TingChina's star and 2016 Olympic champion.
  • Earvin N'GapethFrench showman known for outrageous shots.

Lingo you'll hear

Kill
An attack that scores directly.
Dig
Saving a hard-hit ball just before it lands.
Ace
A serve that scores outright.
Pancake
Saving the ball by sliding a flat hand under it on the floor.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“The setter runs the offense.” The setter is the volleyball equivalent of a quarterback.
