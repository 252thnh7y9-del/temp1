Nordic Combined

Ski jump, then cross-country race. First across the line wins.

Competitors
Individual, plus team events
Format
1 jump + a 10 km cross-country race
Season
November to March
Olympic events
Normal hill, large hill, team

What is it?

Nordic combined pairs two Nordic sports: ski jumping and cross-country skiing. Athletes jump first, and their jump scores are converted into time head starts for the ski race that follows.

That makes for a simple finish: the first skier across the line wins. It's huge in Norway, Germany, Austria and Japan. At the Olympics it is currently a men-only sport.

The basic rules

  1. Each athlete makes a ski jump, scored on distance and style.
  2. Jump scores convert into a time gap: the leader starts the ski race first and the others start behind.
  3. This is called the Gundersen method.
  4. The cross-country race is skied in skate technique.
  5. First across the finish line wins.

Major leagues & competitions

Powerhouse nations

Lingo you'll hear

Gundersen start
The staggered race start based on jump scores.
Normal hill / large hill
The two jump sizes.
Jump points
Distance plus style score.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“He jumped well, but can he hold them off on skis?” The tension is always jumpers vs skiers.
