Horse Racing

The sport of kings: big hats, big bets and two-minute thrills.

Competitors
Usually 5 to 20 horses per race
Race length
About 1–3 minutes on the flat
Season
Year-round, with big festivals in spring and summer
Famous feat
The US Triple Crown

What is it?

In horse racing, jockeys ride thoroughbred horses around a track, and the first past the finish line wins. Most races last only a minute or two, but they're surrounded by pageantry: fancy hats, mint juleps and lots of betting.

There's flat racing, with no obstacles, and jump racing (steeplechase), where horses leap fences. The stars are as much the horses as the people: the owners, trainers and the tiny, fearless jockeys.

The basic rules

  1. The first horse whose nose crosses the finish line wins. Very close races are decided by a photo finish.
  2. Horses may carry extra weight to even out the competition, especially in handicap races.
  3. Fans bet on a horse to win (finish first), place (top two) or show (top three).
  4. The odds show the payout. A 10–1 horse pays $10 for every $1 bet if it wins.
  5. The US Triple Crown means winning the Kentucky Derby, Preakness Stakes and Belmont Stakes, all as a 3-year-old.

Major leagues & competitions

  • Kentucky DerbyFirst Saturday in May. “The most exciting two minutes in sports.”
  • Royal AscotA June festival in England, attended by the royal family.
  • The Grand NationalBritain's famous steeplechase with huge fences.
  • Melbourne Cup“The race that stops a nation,” on the first Tuesday in November.
  • Prix de l'Arc de TriompheEurope's richest race, in Paris.
  • Breeders' CupA two-day championship event in the US.

Legendary horses

  • SecretariatWon the 1973 Triple Crown, taking the Belmont by 31 lengths. Still the record.
  • SeabiscuitA Depression-era underdog hero (and a movie).
  • FrankelUnbeaten in 14 races. Often rated the best racehorse ever.
  • WinxAustralian mare who won 33 races in a row.
  • Red RumWon the Grand National three times.

Famous names to know

  • Frankie DettoriItalian jockey known for his famous flying dismount.
  • Lester PiggottBritish legend with nine Epsom Derby wins.
  • Bill ShoemakerAmerican Hall of Famer with over 8,800 wins.
  • Rachael BlackmoreIrish jockey, the first woman to win the Grand National (2021).

Lingo you'll hear

Furlong
One-eighth of a mile, the standard unit of race distance.
Favorite
The horse with the shortest odds.
Long shot
An unlikely winner with big odds.
Silks
The colorful shirts jockeys wear.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“Never bet on the favorite at the Derby.” Upsets are common, and it sounds savvy.
