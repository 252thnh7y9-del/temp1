Handball

Europe's favorite indoor sport: fast, physical, high-scoring.

Players
7 per team (6 + goalkeeper)
Game length
2 halves × 30 min
Season
September to June in Europe
Biggest events
The Olympics, the World Championship and the EHF Champions League

What is it?

Handball, also called team handball, is played indoors by two teams of seven. Players dribble and pass a small ball with their hands and try to throw it into the other team's goal. It looks like a mix of basketball and soccer.

Teams often score 30 goals or more a game, with spectacular jumping shots. It's huge in Germany, Denmark, France, Spain, Norway and much of Europe, and it's an Olympic sport.

The basic rules

  1. Throw the ball into the goal for 1 point.
  2. You can take at most 3 steps holding the ball and hold it for 3 seconds. Then you must pass, shoot or dribble.
  3. Only the goalkeeper can stand in the 6-meter goal area. Attackers jump into the air above it to shoot.
  4. Defenders may block with their body but can't hit, hold or push.
  5. Serious fouls lead to a 2-minute suspension. Fouls that stop a clear chance give a 7-meter throw (a penalty).
  6. Stalling is penalized as passive play.

Major leagues & competitions

  • EHF Champions LeagueEurope's top club competition, with a Final Four in Cologne.
  • Handball-BundesligaGermany's league, often called the strongest in the world.
  • IHF World ChampionshipEvery two years.
  • Olympic GamesMen's and women's tournaments.

Famous teams

  • France men“Les Experts,” multiple-time Olympic champions.
  • Norway womenA perennial powerhouse.
  • THW KielGermany's most successful club.
  • FC BarcelonaA European club giant.

Famous names to know

  • Nikola KarabatićFrench legend with world, European and Olympic titles.
  • Mikkel HansenDanish star shooter, three-time world player of the year.

Lingo you'll hear

Kempa trick
A pass caught mid-air and shot before landing.
Fast break
A quick counterattack.
7-meter
A penalty throw.
Wing
Players stationed at the sideline corners.

“The goalkeeper's save percentage decides it.” With so many shots, a hot goalie swings games.
