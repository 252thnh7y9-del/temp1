Diving

Flips and twists from 10 meters up, landed with almost no splash.

Competitors
Individual and synchronized pairs
Heights
3 m springboard, 10 m platform
Dive length
About 1.5–2 seconds
Biggest events
The Olympics and the World Aquatics Championships

What is it?

Divers leap from a springboard or a platform, perform flips and twists in the air, and aim to enter the water perfectly vertical with almost no splash.

The whole dive takes under two seconds. China is overwhelmingly dominant: it won all eight diving golds at Paris 2024.

The basic rules

  1. Judges score each dive from 0 to 10 on approach, takeoff, flight and entry.
  2. The score is multiplied by the dive's degree of difficulty.
  3. In synchro, judges also score how well the pair stays in sync.
  4. Divers perform a set number of dives, and the highest total wins.
  5. Body positions are tuck, pike and straight.

Major leagues & competitions

  • Olympic GamesEight events.
  • World Aquatics ChampionshipsThe world championship.
  • World Aquatics Diving World CupA yearly series.

Powerhouse nations

  • ChinaThe “Dream Team,” nearly unbeatable.

  • United StatesHistorically dominant.

Famous names to know

  • Greg LouganisAmerican four-time Olympic champion.
  • Tom DaleyBritish diver who won Olympic synchro gold in 2021.
  • Quan HongchanChinese diver who won gold at 14 with multiple perfect 10s.
  • Guo JingjingChinese “Princess of Diving.”

Lingo you'll hear

Rip entry
A nearly splashless entry.
Degree of difficulty
How hard the dive is.
Armstand
A dive that starts in a handstand.
Belly flop
What you don't want.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“That was a rip entry.” Say it whenever there's barely a splash.
