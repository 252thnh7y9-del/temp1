Australian Rules Football

Australia's own: high-flying marks, kicking and non-stop action.

Players
18 per team on the field
Game length
4 quarters × 20 min plus stoppage time (about 2 hours)
Season
March to September
Big final
The AFL Grand Final

What is it?

Australian rules football, or “footy,” is played on a huge oval field by two teams of 18. Players kick, punch (handball) and run with an oval ball, trying to kick it between the goalposts. There's no offside and the ball can go in any direction, so play flows constantly.

It's Australia's most-watched sport, especially in Melbourne, where the AFL Grand Final at the 100,000-seat MCG is a near-religious event. Its signature moment is the speccy: a player leaping onto opponents' backs to take a spectacular catch.

The basic rules

  1. There are four posts at each end. Kicking between the two tall middle posts is a goal, worth 6 points.
  2. Going between a tall and a short post (or touching a post) is a behind, worth 1 point.
  3. Scores are written like this: 12.8 (80) means 12 goals, 8 behinds, 80 points.
  4. Move the ball by kicking or handballing (punching it off your palm). Throwing isn't allowed.
  5. Catch a kick that travels 15 meters or more and it's a mark: you get a free kick.
  6. When running with the ball, you must bounce it (or touch it to the ground) every 15 meters.

Major leagues & competitions

  • AFLAustralian Football League. 18 professional teams.
  • AFLWThe women's professional league, launched in 2017.
  • State leagues (VFL, SANFL, WAFL)Second-tier competitions.

Famous teams

  • Collingwood MagpiesThe biggest and most polarizing club. Fans love them or hate them.
  • Carlton BluesOne of the oldest and most successful clubs.
  • Essendon BombersA historic Melbourne powerhouse.
  • Hawthorn HawksWon three straight premierships, 2013–2015.
  • Brisbane LionsThe leading club outside Victoria, with recent premierships.

Famous names to know

  • Leigh MatthewsVoted the greatest player of the 20th century.
  • Gary Ablett Jr.A dazzling midfielder and two-time Brownlow Medal winner.
  • Lance “Buddy” FranklinSuperstar goal kicker and crowd favorite.
  • Tony LockettHolds the record for most career goals.
  • Dustin MartinWon three Grand Final best-on-ground medals.

Lingo you'll hear

Mark
A clean catch from a kick, which earns a free kick.
Speccy
A spectacular leaping mark.
Premiership
The championship.
Brownlow Medal
Award for the league's best and fairest player.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“Carn the [team name]!” “Carn” is footy-speak for “come on.”
