Speed Skating

Pairs racing the clock on a 400-meter oval of ice.

Competitors
Pairs racing the clock, plus mass start and team pursuit
Distances
500 m up to 10,000 m
Season
November to March
Biggest events
The Olympics and the World Championships

What is it?

Long track speed skaters race around a 400-meter oval in pairs, against the clock. With long, powerful strides and the athletes crouched low, they reach speeds over 60 km/h (37 mph).

The Netherlands is crazy about it: Dutch fans in orange fill the stands, and the Dutch win more Olympic medals than any other nation.

The basic rules

  1. Two skaters race at once in separate lanes. Each lap, they switch lanes on the crossing straight.
  2. The fastest time wins, regardless of your pairing.
  3. In team pursuit, two teams of three start on opposite sides of the oval.
  4. The mass start is a 16-lap pack race with sprint points.
  5. Two false starts lead to disqualification.
