Street culture meets Olympic gold, with teenage champions.

What is it?

Skateboarding joined the Olympics in 2020. Skaters perform tricks in two formats: street, a course of stairs, rails and ledges like a city plaza, and park, a bowl of smooth curved walls.

Many of its Olympic champions are teenagers, some as young as 13. Japan has become a skateboarding powerhouse.

The basic rules

  1. Street: skaters do timed runs plus single trick attempts. The best scores count.
  2. Park: 45-second runs in a bowl. The best run counts.
  3. Judges score difficulty, execution, variety, speed and flow.
  4. Falling usually ruins a run or trick score.
  5. Originality impresses judges.
