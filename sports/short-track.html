Short Track Speed Skating

Roller derby on ice: tight turns, pack racing and crashes.

Competitors
4–6 skaters per race
Track
111.12 m oval inside an ice rink
Race distances
500 m, 1000 m, 1500 m, plus relays
Biggest events
The Olympics and the World Championships

What is it?

In short track, four to six skaters race together around a small oval inside a hockey rink. Leaning into tight turns, they jostle for position and pass at high speed. Crashes and disqualifications are common, so the favorite often doesn't win.

It's one of the most popular Winter Olympic sports in South Korea, China, Canada and the Netherlands.

The basic rules

  1. Skaters advance through heats, quarterfinals and semifinals to an A final.
  2. Place matters, not time: the first across the line wins.
  3. Impeding, pushing or cutting off other skaters leads to disqualification.
  4. Skaters knocked down by a foul may be advanced to the next round.
  5. Relays have teammates tagging in by pushing the next skater from behind.

Lingo you'll hear

Winning unexpectedly when others fail.
Penalty

Inside pass
The riskiest, most exciting overtake.
