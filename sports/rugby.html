Rugby

Football's tougher cousin: no pads, only backward passes.

Players
15 per team (7 in Olympic “rugby sevens”)
Game length
2 halves × 40 min
Season
Roughly September to June in Europe
Biggest events
The Rugby World Cup and the Six Nations

What is it?

Rugby, here meaning rugby union, the most popular version, is a full-contact game where two teams of 15 carry, pass and kick an oval ball toward the opponent's goal line. The play flows continuously, and the players wear no helmets or pads.

It's a national passion in New Zealand, South Africa, Wales, Ireland, France, England, Fiji and Argentina. Despite the brutal hits, rugby is famous for its sportsmanship: players respect the referee and shake hands after the game.

The basic rules

  1. You can run with the ball or kick it forward, but you can only pass backward (or sideways).
  2. Try (touch the ball down over the opponent's goal line): 5 points. Unlike in American football, you must ground it.
  3. Conversion (a kick through the posts after a try): 2 points. Penalty kick or drop goal: 3 points.
  4. When a player is tackled, they must release the ball, and both teams compete for it in a ruck.
  5. A scrum (the big pushing huddle) restarts play after minor mistakes. A lineout restarts it when the ball goes out the side.
  6. Tackles must be below the shoulders. Dangerous play can get a yellow card (10 minutes off) or a red card.

Major leagues & competitions

  • Rugby World CupEvery four years. The biggest event in the sport.
  • Six NationsYearly tournament: England, France, Ireland, Italy, Scotland and Wales.
  • The Rugby ChampionshipSouthern hemisphere: New Zealand, South Africa, Australia and Argentina.
  • Super Rugby / Premiership / Top 14Top club leagues in the southern hemisphere, England and France.
  • Rugby League (NRL)A related 13-a-side sport, huge in Australia.

Famous teams

  • New Zealand “All Blacks”Legendary winners who perform the haka war dance before games.
  • South Africa “Springboks”Four-time World Cup winners, a record.
  • EnglandWon the 2003 World Cup.
  • IrelandA top-ranked side in recent years.
  • ToulouseFrance's most decorated club.

Famous names to know

  • Jonah LomuGiant, unstoppable All Blacks winger who made rugby a global spectacle.
  • Richie McCawAll Blacks captain who won two World Cups.
  • Dan CarterPerhaps the best fly-half (playmaker and kicker) ever.
  • Siya KolisiSouth Africa's first Black Test captain, and a two-time World Cup winner.
  • Antoine DupontFrench scrum-half, considered the best player in the world today.

Lingo you'll hear

Try
Rugby's touchdown.
Haka
The Māori challenge the All Blacks perform before kickoff.
Knock-on
Dropping the ball forward. That gives a scrum to the other team.
Sin bin
10 minutes off the field after a yellow card.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“The game is won up front.” It means the big forwards in the scrum decide matches.
