Ice Hockey

The fastest team sport, played on knives on ice.

Players on the ice
6 per team (5 skaters + a goalie)
Game length
3 periods × 20 min (about 2.5 hours real time)
Season
October to June
Big final
The Stanley Cup Final

What is it?

Ice hockey is played on skates. Players use sticks to shoot a hard rubber disc, the puck, into the other team's net. It is extremely fast, and players change on the fly every 45 seconds or so because skating at that speed is exhausting.

Hockey is Canada's national obsession and hugely popular in the northern US, Scandinavia, Russia and Central Europe. Its trophy, the Stanley Cup, is one of the oldest in pro sports, and each winning player gets to spend a day with it.

The basic rules

  1. Get the puck into the net for 1 goal. Most goals wins.
  2. Offside: attackers can't enter the opponent's zone (past the blue line) before the puck does.
  3. Icing: you can't just fire the puck the length of the ice to clear it. Play stops and restarts near your own goal.
  4. Penalties (tripping, high-sticking and so on) send a player to the penalty box, usually for 2 minutes. The other team gets a power play with an extra skater.
  5. Body checking (hitting with the shoulder or hip) is legal in men's pro hockey. Fighting earns a 5-minute penalty but is still tolerated.
  6. Regular-season ties go to overtime and then a shootout. Playoff games use sudden-death overtime until someone scores.

Major leagues & competitions

  • NHLNational Hockey League. 32 teams across the US and Canada.
  • PWHLProfessional Women's Hockey League, launched in 2024.
  • KHLRussia-based league, the second strongest in the world.
  • Winter OlympicsWhere countries like Canada, the US, Sweden and Finland battle for gold.

Famous teams

  • Montreal Canadiens24 Stanley Cups, the most of any team.
  • Toronto Maple LeafsHugely popular, and famous for a Cup drought dating to 1967.
  • Detroit Red Wings“Hockeytown,” where fans throw octopuses on the ice.
  • Boston BruinsOne of the “Original Six” founding teams, known for toughness.
  • Edmonton OilersWayne Gretzky's dynasty of the 1980s.

Famous names to know

  • Wayne Gretzky“The Great One.” Holds dozens of records and is the clear GOAT.
  • Alex OvechkinRussian sniper who broke Gretzky's career goals record in 2025.
  • Sidney CrosbyCanadian captain and three-time Cup winner with Pittsburgh.
  • Connor McDavidConsidered the fastest and most skilled player of today.
  • Mario LemieuxPittsburgh legend who also later owned the team.

Lingo you'll hear

Hat trick
Three goals by one player. Fans throw hats onto the ice.
Power play
Having more players because the other team took a penalty.
Five-hole
The gap between a goalie's legs.
Playoff beard
Players don't shave during the playoffs.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“The goalie stood on his head.” It means the goalie played amazingly. Say it after any close win.
