Alpine Skiing

Racing straight down a mountain at highway speeds.

Competitors
Individual, one at a time
Race length
About 45 seconds to 2.5 minutes
Season
October to March (World Cup)
Olympic events
Downhill, super-G, giant slalom, slalom, team combined

What is it?

Alpine skiing is downhill racing. One at a time, skiers race down a mountain course marked with gates (pairs of poles or flags), and the fastest time wins. Races are often decided by hundredths of a second.

The events range from downhill, the fastest, where skiers top 130 km/h (80 mph), to slalom, a short course of tight, rapid turns. It's a national obsession in Austria and Switzerland.

The basic rules

  1. Skiers start one at a time, and the clock decides the winner.
  2. You must pass through every gate. Missing one means disqualification.
  3. Downhill and super-G are speed events with one run each.
  4. Giant slalom and slalom are technical events with two runs on different courses. The times are added together.
  5. Crashes are common. Skiers who don't finish are listed as DNF.

Major leagues & competitions

  • Winter OlympicsThe biggest stage.
  • FIS Alpine World CupA season-long series. The overall winner gets a big crystal globe.
  • World ChampionshipsEvery two years.
  • Kitzbühel (Hahnenkamm)The most feared and famous downhill race, in Austria.

Powerhouse nations

  • AustriaSkiing is the national sport.
  • SwitzerlandAustria's great rival.
  • NorwayKnown for its tight-knit, successful men's team.
  • United StatesProduced superstars like Shiffrin and Vonn.

Famous names to know

  • Mikaela ShiffrinAmerican with more World Cup wins than any skier in history.
  • Lindsey VonnAmerican downhill legend who made a comeback in her 40s with a titanium knee.
  • Marcel HirscherAustrian who won eight straight overall World Cup titles.
  • Ingemar StenmarkSwedish slalom legend of the 1970s and 1980s.
  • Marco OdermattSwiss star who dominated the men's World Cup in the 2020s.

Lingo you'll hear

Gate
The markers skiers must pass through.
Tuck
The crouched, aerodynamic position used at high speed.
Crystal globe
The trophy for season-long World Cup titles.
Straddle
Skiing with one ski on each side of a gate, which disqualifies you.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“The Hahnenkamm is the scariest race in the world.” Every skier agrees.
