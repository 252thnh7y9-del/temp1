Curling

Chess on ice, with brooms. Weirdly addictive to watch.

Players
4 per team (2 per team in mixed doubles)
Game length
8–10 “ends” (about 2–3 hours)
Stone weight
About 20 kg (44 lb)
Biggest events
Winter Olympics, World Championships, the Brier

What is it?

In curling, two teams of four take turns sliding heavy granite stones down a sheet of ice toward a bullseye target called the house. Teammates sweep the ice in front of the moving stone with brooms, which helps it travel farther and straighter.

It's a game of precision and strategy: knocking out opponents' stones, guarding your own and setting up the final shot. Curling is hugely popular in Canada and Scotland, where it was born, and it's a cult favorite at every Winter Olympics.

The basic rules

  1. A game is split into ends, a bit like innings. In each end, every player throws 2 stones, so each team throws 8.
  2. After all 16 stones are thrown, only one team scores: the team with the stone closest to the center.
  3. That team scores 1 point for each of its stones closer to the center than the opponent's best stone.
  4. Having the last throw of an end, the hammer, is a big advantage. The team that doesn't score usually gets the hammer next end.
  5. Sweeping warms the ice slightly, so the stone slides farther and curls less.
  6. Teams often concede (shake hands) when they can't catch up. It's considered good sportsmanship.

Major leagues & competitions

  • Winter OlympicsMen's, women's and mixed doubles events.
  • World Curling ChampionshipsHeld every year.
  • The Brier / Scotties Tournament of HeartsCanada's national men's and women's championships, huge TV events there.
  • Grand Slam of CurlingA series of top professional events.

Powerhouse nations

  • CanadaThe giant of the sport, with more than a million curlers.
  • SwedenNiklas Edin's team dominated the men's game for a decade.
  • Scotland / Great BritainThe birthplace of curling. Most stones are made from Scottish granite.
  • SwitzerlandConsistent medal winners.
  • JapanWomen's team “Loco Solare” became national celebrities.

Famous names to know

  • Niklas EdinSwedish skip with multiple world titles and 2022 Olympic gold.
  • Jennifer JonesCanadian skip who went unbeaten to win 2014 Olympic gold.
  • Kevin MartinCanadian legend and 2010 Olympic champion.
  • Eve MuirheadScottish skip who led Great Britain to 2022 Olympic gold.
  • Bruce MouatScotland's top men's skip of the 2020s.

Lingo you'll hear

Skip
The team captain, who calls the shots and holds the broom as a target.
Hammer
The last stone of the end.
Button
The very center of the house.
Hurry hard!
The famous yell telling sweepers to sweep faster.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“They should blank the end to keep the hammer.” A blank end (nobody scores) is a classic strategic move.
