What is it?

Sport climbing joined the Olympics in 2020. Athletes climb artificial walls with colored holds in three disciplines. Boulder is short, tricky climbs without ropes. Lead means climbing as high as possible on a tall wall. Speed is a head-to-head race up a standard wall.

Athletes see the boulder and lead routes for the first time only minutes before they compete, so problem-solving is as important as strength.

The basic rules

  1. Boulder: climbers get points for reaching the top or a midpoint “zone” hold, with fewer attempts scoring higher.
  2. Lead: points for how high you climb before falling, with a time limit.
  3. Speed: two climbers race side by side on an identical route. Fastest wins.
  4. Before boulder and lead rounds, climbers get a short preview of the routes.
  5. In speed, a false start means disqualification.
