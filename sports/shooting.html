Shooting

Perfect stillness under pressure. Rifle, pistol and shotgun.

Competitors
Individual and mixed team
Disciplines
Rifle, pistol, shotgun (trap and skeet)
Distances
10 m, 25 m and 50 m
Biggest events
The Olympics and the World Championships

What is it?

Olympic shooting tests precision and nerves. Athletes fire rifles and pistols at targets, or shotguns at flying clay discs, aiming for perfection while controlling their breath and heartbeat.

It has been an Olympic sport since 1896. At Paris 2024 it produced unlikely viral stars, including a Turkish shooter who won silver with one hand casually in his pocket.

The basic rules

  1. Rifle and pistol: shoot at a ringed target. Each shot scores up to 10.9 in finals.
  2. Finals eliminate the lowest scorer step by step.
  3. Trap and skeet: shoot clay targets launched into the air. Each hit is a point.
  4. Strict safety rules apply on the range.
  5. Mixed team events pair a man and a woman.
