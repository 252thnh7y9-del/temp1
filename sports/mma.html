MMA (Mixed Martial Arts)

Striking plus wrestling, in a cage. The UFC made it mainstream.

Competitors
1 vs 1, in weight classes
Fight length
3 rounds × 5 min (5 rounds for title fights)
Season
Year-round, with events most weekends
Biggest promotion
The UFC

What is it?

Mixed martial arts combines techniques from boxing, kickboxing, wrestling, judo and jiu-jitsu. Fighters can punch, kick, knee and elbow standing up, then take the fight to the ground and grapple for a submission hold.

The UFC (Ultimate Fighting Championship) started in 1993 as a “which style is best?” spectacle and grew into a global sport. Fights take place in an eight-sided cage called the Octagon.

The basic rules

  1. Win by knockout or TKO: the opponent is knocked out, or the referee stops the fight.
  2. Win by submission: the opponent “taps out” (taps the mat or you) to give up during a choke or joint lock.
  3. Otherwise, three judges score each round, as in boxing.
  4. Banned moves include eye gouging, groin strikes, strikes to the back of the head and spine, biting, and kicking or kneeing the head of a downed opponent.
  5. Fighters wear small, fingerless gloves so they can grab and grapple.

Major leagues & competitions

  • UFCBy far the biggest and richest promotion.
  • PFLProfessional Fighters League, which uses a season-and-playoffs format.
  • ONE ChampionshipThe leading promotion in Asia.

Famous gyms

  • American Top TeamA huge Florida gym that has produced many champions.
  • Jackson Wink MMAAn Albuquerque gym famous for its fight strategy.
  • American Kickboxing Academy (AKA)California home of Khabib and other Dagestani stars.

Famous names to know

  • Conor McGregorIrish showman, the sport's biggest star and first two-division UFC champ.
  • Jon JonesOften called the most talented fighter ever.
  • Khabib NurmagomedovRetired unbeaten at 29–0.
  • Ronda RouseyThe first UFC women's champion, who brought women's MMA mainstream.
  • Georges St-PierreCanadian legend, respected as one of the greatest.
  • Amanda NunesBrazilian two-division champion.

Lingo you'll hear

Tap out
To give up during a submission hold.
Ground and pound
Punching an opponent while on top of them on the ground.
Rear-naked choke
The most common submission, a choke from behind.
Fight camp
The months of training before a fight.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“Wrestling wins fights.” Wrestlers often control where a fight takes place.
