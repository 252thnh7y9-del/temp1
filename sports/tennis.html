Tennis

One-on-one combat with rackets and strange scoring.

Players
1 vs 1 (singles) or 2 vs 2 (doubles)
Match length
Best of 3 sets (best of 5 for men at Grand Slams)
Season
January to November, all around the world
Biggest events
The four Grand Slams

What is it?

In tennis, two players (or two pairs, in doubles) hit a ball back and forth over a net with rackets. You win a point when your opponent can't return the ball into the court. There's no clock: a match lasts until someone wins, which can take an hour or five.

The sport revolves around four huge tournaments, the Grand Slams. They are played on different surfaces (hard courts, clay and grass), and each one rewards a different style of play.

The basic rules

  1. Points within a game go 0 (“love”), 15, 30, 40, game. Yes, really.
  2. At 40–40 (deuce), a player must win two points in a row. The first one gives them the advantage.
  3. Win 6 games (by at least 2) to win a set. At 6–6, a tiebreak usually decides it.
  4. The server gets two tries to land the serve in the diagonal box. Missing both is a double fault.
  5. The ball may bounce only once on your side. A ball landing on the line counts as in.
  6. Players switch serving every game and switch ends of the court every two games.

Major leagues & competitions

  • ATP TourThe men's professional tour.
  • WTA TourThe women's professional tour.
  • Davis Cup / Billie Jean King CupTeam competitions between countries.

The big four tournaments

  • Australian OpenMelbourne, January. Hard courts and serious heat.
  • French Open (Roland-Garros)Paris, late May to June. Slow red clay.
  • WimbledonLondon, July. Grass, an all-white dress code, strawberries and cream.
  • US OpenNew York, late August to September. Loud night sessions under the lights.

Famous names to know

  • Serena Williams23 Grand Slam singles titles and a cultural icon.
  • Roger FedererSwiss maestro, admired for his graceful style.
  • Rafael NadalSpanish “King of Clay” with a record 14 French Open titles.
  • Novak DjokovicSerbian with the most men's Grand Slam titles ever.
  • Carlos AlcarazSpanish young star with a flashy, exciting style.
  • Jannik SinnerItalian star and Alcaraz's great modern rival.

Lingo you'll hear

Ace
A serve the opponent can't even touch.
Break
Winning a game while your opponent serves.
Love
Zero.
Grand Slam
One of the four majors, or winning all four in one year.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“Serve is so important on grass.” It's true at Wimbledon, and it sounds very knowledgeable.
