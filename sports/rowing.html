Rowing (Crew)

Eight rowers, one rhythm, total exhaustion. The original college sport.

Crew size
1, 2, 4 or 8 rowers (plus a coxswain in eights)
Race length
2,000 m, about 5.5–7 minutes
Season
Fall: long “head” races. Spring: 2,000 m sprints.
Biggest events
The Olympics, Henley Royal Regatta and the Boat Race

What is it?

In rowing, often called “crew” at US colleges, athletes race long, narrow boats across the water using oars. Rowers face backward, so a small coxswain sits at the stern, facing forward, to steer and shout instructions.

Rowing is the oldest intercollegiate sport in the US: Harvard and Yale first raced in 1852. It's a strong Olympic sport, especially for Great Britain, and inspired the bestseller The Boys in the Boat.

The basic rules

  1. In side-by-side sprint races (spring season), the first bow across the line wins.
  2. In head races (fall season), boats start one after another and race against the clock.
  3. Sweep rowing: each rower holds one oar. Sculling: each rower holds two.
  4. The coxswain steers, calls the stroke rate and motivates the crew.
  5. The stroke seat, closest to the stern, sets the rhythm everyone follows.

Major leagues & competitions

  • NCAA women's rowingAn official NCAA championship sport.
  • IRA ChampionshipThe national championship for US men's college rowing.
  • Head of the Charles (Boston)The world's largest two-day regatta, every October.
  • The Boat RaceOxford vs Cambridge on the Thames, since 1829.
  • Henley Royal RegattaA historic English regatta full of blazers and Pimm's.

Famous teams

  • University of WashingtonIts 1936 crew won Olympic gold in Berlin, the subject of The Boys in the Boat.
  • Harvard & YaleRivals in the oldest US college race, since 1852.
  • Oxford & CambridgeThe famous Boat Race rivalry.
  • Cal Golden BearsA West Coast powerhouse.
  • Great Britain national teamAmong the most successful Olympic rowing nations.

Famous names to know

  • Sir Steve RedgraveBritish rower who won gold at five straight Olympics.
  • Joe RantzOne of “The Boys in the Boat.”
  • Sir Matthew PinsentRedgrave's partner, with four Olympic golds.
  • Helen GloverBritish two-time Olympic champion who returned after having three children.

Lingo you'll hear

Erg
The rowing machine, known for brutal fitness tests.
Catching a crab
When an oar gets stuck in the water, sometimes throwing the rower out of the boat.
Power ten
Ten all-out strokes called by the coxswain.
Regatta
A rowing race event.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“The cox is the brains of the boat.” Every rower will agree.
