Weightlifting

Two lifts, three attempts each, and the heaviest total wins.

Competitors
Individual, in weight classes
Lifts
Snatch, and clean and jerk

Biggest events
The Olympics and the World Championships

What is it?

Olympic weightlifting tests explosive strength and technique. Lifters attempt two lifts: the snatch, where the bar goes overhead in one motion, and the clean and jerk, where the bar goes to the shoulders and then overhead.

Top super-heavyweights lift over 200 kg (440 lb) in the snatch alone.

The basic rules

  1. Each lifter gets 3 attempts at each lift.
  2. The best successful snatch plus the best clean and jerk is your total.
  3. The heaviest total wins.
  4. Three referees judge each lift with white (good) or red (no lift) lights.
  5. Failing all three attempts in one lift means a bomb out: no total.
