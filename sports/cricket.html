Cricket

Bat and ball, beloved by billions, and sometimes 5 days long.

Players
11 per team
Game length
T20: ~3 hours · One-Day: ~8 hours · Test: up to 5 days
Season
Year-round, moving around the globe
Biggest events
The Cricket World Cup, the Ashes and the IPL

What is it?

Cricket is a bat-and-ball game played by two teams of 11. One team bats and tries to score runs, while the other bowls and fields and tries to get batters out. Then they swap. It's huge in India, Pakistan, Australia, England, South Africa, the Caribbean and beyond, with perhaps more than a billion fans.

It comes in different lengths. Traditional Test matches last up to five days and can still end in a draw. The modern T20 format finishes in about three hours and is full of big hitting.

The basic rules

  1. A bowler delivers the ball, usually with one bounce, toward a wicket (three wooden stumps). The batter defends it.
  2. Batters score runs by running between the two wickets after hitting the ball.
  3. Hitting the ball over the boundary rope along the ground scores 4. Clearing it in the air scores 6.
  4. Main ways to get out: bowled (the ball hits the stumps), caught (a fielder catches the hit in the air), LBW (the ball would have hit the stumps but hit the batter's leg) and run out.
  5. An over is 6 balls from one bowler. T20 gives each team 20 overs, and One-Day matches give 50.
  6. An innings ends when 10 batters are out or the overs run out. The team with more runs wins.

Major leagues & competitions

  • Indian Premier League (IPL)A glitzy T20 league and one of the richest competitions in all of sports.
  • ICC Cricket World CupThe 50-over world championship, every four years.
  • ICC T20 World CupThe short-format world championship.
  • The AshesThe historic Test rivalry between England and Australia.
  • Big Bash League / The HundredPopular short-format leagues in Australia and England.

Famous teams

  • IndiaA cricket-mad nation, and the sport's financial powerhouse.
  • AustraliaThe most successful World Cup team in history.
  • EnglandWhere the game was invented.
  • Mumbai IndiansOne of the most successful IPL teams.
  • Chennai Super KingsA hugely popular IPL team, long led by MS Dhoni.

Famous names to know

  • Sachin TendulkarIndian “Little Master,” scorer of 100 international centuries.
  • Virat KohliModern Indian superstar with an enormous social media following.
  • Don BradmanAustralian whose batting average of 99.94 is considered the greatest stat in any sport.
  • MS DhoniCalm Indian captain who won the 2011 World Cup.
  • Shane WarneAustralian spin-bowling genius.
  • Brian LaraWest Indies legend with a record 400 runs in one Test innings.

Lingo you'll hear

Century
100 runs by one batter.
Duck
Getting out for zero.
Howzat?
“How's that?” Fielders shout it to appeal for an out.
Pitch
The strip in the middle where the action happens.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“Test cricket is the real test.” Traditionalists will love you for saying it.
