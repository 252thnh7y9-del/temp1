Formula 1

Motorsport's top tier: 200 mph, global glamour and team drama.

Competitors
10–11 teams with 2 drivers each
Race length
About 305 km, roughly 1.5–2 hours
Season
March to December, about 24 races
Prize
The Drivers' and Constructors' (team) championships

What is it?

Formula 1 is the highest level of single-seat auto racing. About 20 drivers race purpose-built cars, among the most advanced machines on Earth, around tracks and city streets worldwide, from Monaco to Las Vegas.

It's a team sport as much as a driving one. Each team builds its own car, and engineering, strategy and pit stops matter as much as the driver. Netflix's Drive to Survive introduced millions of new fans to the sport.

The basic rules

  1. Each race weekend has practice, then qualifying: a fastest-lap shootout that sets the starting order (the grid). First place is pole position.
  2. The top 10 finishers earn points, with 25 for a win. Points add up over the season.
  3. Two titles are decided: the Drivers' Championship for the best driver and the Constructors' Championship for the best team.
  4. Cars must make at least one pit stop to change tires. Tire choice and timing is a key strategy.
  5. Flags: yellow means danger, slow down. Red means the race is stopped. Checkered means the finish. A safety car slows the field after crashes.
  6. Teammates drive identical cars, so your teammate is also your biggest rival.

Major leagues & competitions

  • Formula 1The top international series.
  • Formula 2 / Formula 3Junior series where future F1 drivers come from.
  • IndyCarAmerica's open-wheel series, home of the Indianapolis 500.
  • NASCARAmerican stock-car racing, hugely popular in the US South.
  • F1 AcademyA series for female drivers.

Famous teams

  • FerrariThe oldest and most iconic team. Its passionate fans are called the tifosi.
  • MercedesWon eight straight Constructors' titles from 2014 to 2021.
  • Red Bull RacingEnergy drink company turned title-winning powerhouse.
  • McLarenA historic British team, back at the front in the mid-2020s.
  • WilliamsA legendary independent team with nine Constructors' titles.

Famous names to know

  • Lewis HamiltonBritish driver with 7 world titles, a record he shares. Joined Ferrari in 2025.
  • Michael SchumacherGerman legend with 7 titles, mostly with Ferrari.
  • Ayrton SennaBrazilian icon, considered by many the most naturally gifted ever.
  • Max VerstappenDutch driver who dominated the early 2020s with Red Bull.
  • Lando NorrisPopular British driver for McLaren.
  • Charles LeclercMonaco-born Ferrari star.
