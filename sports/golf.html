Golf

A long walk where the lowest score wins.

Players
Individual, usually grouped in twos or threes
Tournament length
4 rounds of 18 holes, Thursday to Sunday
Season
Year-round, with the biggest events April to July
Biggest events
The four majors and the Ryder Cup

What is it?

In golf, players hit a small ball into a series of holes, usually 18, spread across a large outdoor course. The goal is to use as few strokes (swings) as possible. Players mostly compete against the course rather than against each other directly.

Pro tournaments run four days. TV coverage hops between players around the course, and the tension builds on Sunday afternoon as the leaders reach the final holes.

The basic rules

  1. Each hole has a par, the number of strokes a skilled golfer should need (3, 4 or 5).
  2. One under par is a birdie, two under is an eagle, and one over is a bogey.
  3. Scores are shown relative to par, so “−10” means 10 strokes under par for the tournament. Lower is better.
  4. Players may carry at most 14 clubs. Drivers hit far, irons are for precision, and putters roll the ball on the green.
  5. You play the ball where it lies. Hitting it into water or out of bounds costs penalty strokes.
  6. Golf runs on etiquette: stay quiet during a swing, and players often call penalties on themselves.

Major leagues & competitions

  • PGA TourThe main men's professional tour, based in the US.
  • LPGA TourThe top women's tour.
  • DP World TourThe European-based men's tour.
  • LIV GolfSaudi-funded rival league with team events, launched in 2022.

The big events

  • The MastersApril, at Augusta National. The winner gets a green jacket.
  • PGA ChampionshipMay, at a different US course each year.
  • U.S. OpenJune. Known for brutally hard courses.
  • The Open ChampionshipJuly, on windy seaside links courses in the UK.
  • Ryder CupUSA vs Europe team event every two years. Fans get rowdy.

Famous names to know

  • Tiger Woods15 majors and the reason golf became cool for a generation.
  • Jack Nicklaus“The Golden Bear,” with a record 18 majors.
  • Arnold Palmer1960s icon, also the name of the iced tea and lemonade drink.
  • Rory McIlroyNorthern Irish star who completed the career Grand Slam in 2025.
  • Scottie SchefflerThe dominant world No. 1 of the mid-2020s.
  • Annika SörenstamSwedish legend of women's golf.

Lingo you'll hear

Hole in one
Getting the ball in with a single shot.
Fore!
Shouted to warn people that a ball is flying their way.
Fairway
The short, neat grass leading to the green.
Caddie
The person who carries the bag and gives advice.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“Drive for show, putt for dough.” It means the short shots on the green decide who wins.
