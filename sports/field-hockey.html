Field Hockey

Huge worldwide: sticks, a hard ball and lightning-fast skills.

Players
11 per team, including a goalkeeper
Game length
4 quarters × 15 min
Season
Varies. US college hockey is in the fall.
Biggest events
The Olympics and the Hockey World Cup

What is it?

Field hockey, called just “hockey” in most of the world, is played on grass or artificial turf by two teams of 11. Players use hooked sticks to dribble, pass and shoot a hard ball into the goal.

It's a major sport in India, Pakistan, the Netherlands, Australia, Argentina, Germany and Belgium. In the US it's mostly played by women at high school and college level.

The basic rules

  1. You can only play the ball with the flat side of the stick.
  2. Goals count only if shot from inside the shooting circle (the “D”) in front of goal.
  3. Defensive fouls in the circle give the attackers a penalty corner, a set play that is a big scoring chance.
  4. Field players can't use their feet or body to control the ball.
  5. Cards: green is a 2-minute suspension, yellow is 5 to 10 minutes and red means the player is off for good.

Major leagues & competitions

  • Olympic GamesMen's and women's tournaments.
  • FIH Hockey World CupEvery four years.
  • FIH Pro LeagueA yearly league between top nations.
  • HoofdklasseThe Netherlands' top club league, probably the strongest in the world.
  • NCAA field hockeyPopular in US colleges.

Famous teams

  • India menA record 8 Olympic golds.
  • Netherlands womenThe dominant team of the modern era.
  • Australia “Kookaburras”A perennial men's powerhouse.
  • Argentina women “Las Leonas”A beloved national team.

Famous names to know

  • Dhyan ChandIndia's “Wizard” and 3-time Olympic champion. India's National Sports Day is his birthday.
  • Luciana AymarArgentine, named world's best player a record 8 times.
  • Teun de NooijerDutch legend and double Olympic champion.
  • Jamie DwyerAustralian star, five-time world player of the year.

Lingo you'll hear

The D
The shooting circle.
Drag flick
A powerful scooped shot used on penalty corners.
Short corner
Another name for a penalty corner.
Reverse stick
Hitting with the stick turned over.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“They need to convert their corners.” Penalty corners often decide games.
