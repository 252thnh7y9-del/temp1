Taekwondo

Korea's martial art: spinning kicks scored by sensors.

What is it?

Taekwondo is a Korean martial art known for high, fast and spinning kicks. Olympic athletes wear electronic body protectors and headgear with sensors that register valid kicks.

Kicks to the head and spinning kicks score the most, so matches feature spectacular moves.

The basic rules

  1. Punch to the body: 1 point. Kick to the body: 2. Spinning kick to the body: 4.
  2. Kick to the head: 3. Spinning kick to the head: 5.
  3. Punches to the head aren't allowed.
  4. Penalties (gam-jeom) give a point to the opponent.
  5. Win 2 rounds to win the match.
