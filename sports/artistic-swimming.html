Artistic Swimming

Underwater gymnastics and dance, performed in perfect sync.

Competitors
Teams of 8 and duets
Routine length
About 2–4 minutes
Season
Year-round
Biggest events
The Olympics and the World Aquatics Championships

What is it?

Artistic swimming, formerly called synchronized swimming, combines swimming, dance and gymnastics. Teams perform choreographed routines to music, with lifts and throws and swimmers moving in perfect unison, all without touching the bottom of the pool.

It looks graceful, but it's extraordinarily demanding: swimmers hold their breath for long stretches while working at full effort upside down.

The basic rules

  1. Routines are scored by judges on execution, artistic impression and difficulty.
  2. Swimmers can't touch the bottom of the pool.
  3. Teams declare their planned elements in advance, and judges check that they perform them correctly.
  4. Nose clips are worn to stop water entering the nose while upside down.
  5. Men became eligible for the Olympic team event in 2024.

Lingo you'll hear

Lift
Teammates launching a swimmer into the air.
Eggbeater
The treading kick that keeps swimmers high.

Choreography performed before entering the pool.
