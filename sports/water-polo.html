Water Polo

Soccer meets swimming meets wrestling, without touching the bottom.

Players
7 per team (6 + goalie)
Game length
4 quarters × 8 min of play
Shot clock
30 seconds per possession (under current rules)
Biggest events
The Olympics and the World Aquatics Championships

What is it?

Water polo is a team sport played in a deep pool. Two teams of seven (six field players and a goalie) try to throw a ball into the other team's goal. Players can't touch the bottom or the sides, so they tread water for the whole game using a powerful kick called the eggbeater.

It's one of the most physically demanding sports in the world. Much of the action, including grabbing, holding and kicking, happens underwater where referees can't see it. It's an Olympic sport and especially big in Hungary, Serbia, Italy, Croatia and California.

The basic rules

  1. Throw the ball into the opponent's goal for 1 point.
  2. Field players may only touch the ball with one hand at a time. The goalie can use both.
  3. No touching the bottom of the pool or holding the side.
  4. A minor foul gives the other team a free throw. A major foul gets the offender an exclusion: 20 seconds out, like a hockey penalty.
  5. A foul that stops a likely goal results in a penalty shot from 5 meters.
  6. A player with three major fouls is out for the rest of the game.

Famous teams

  • HungaryThe most Olympic golds in men's water polo, and a national passion.
  • SerbiaWon three straight men's Olympic golds (2016, 2020, 2024).
  • USA womenWon three straight Olympic golds from 2012 to 2020.
  • Pro ReccoItalian club with a record number of European titles.

Famous names to know

  • Dezső GyarmatiHungarian legend with three Olympic golds.
  • Maggie SteffensUSA women's captain and three-time Olympic champion.
  • Filip FilipovićSerbian star and two-time Olympic champion.
  • Tony AzevedoAmerican who played in five Olympics.

Lingo you'll hear

Eggbeater
The circular treading kick that keeps players high in the water.
Exclusion
20 seconds in the penalty area.
Man-up
Having an extra player because of an exclusion.
Lob
A soft arcing shot over the goalie.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“Half the game happens underwater.” Players grab and kick below the surface where referees can't see.
