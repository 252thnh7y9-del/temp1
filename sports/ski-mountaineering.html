Ski Mountaineering

Ski up the mountain, run with skis on your back, then ski down.

Competitors
Individual and mixed relay at the Olympics
Olympic race length
A few minutes for the sprint
Season
December to April
Olympic debut
2026

What is it?

Ski mountaineering (“skimo”) races go uphill as well as down. Athletes climb on skis fitted with grippy skins, scramble up steep sections on foot with skis on their backs, then rip off the skins and race downhill.

It comes from Alpine mountaineering culture and made its Olympic debut at the 2026 Milan Cortina Games.

The basic rules

  1. Races include uphill skiing (on skins), a hiking section on foot and a downhill run.
  2. Transitions, switching between modes, happen in marked zones and are a key skill.
  3. First across the finish line wins.
  4. The Olympic sprint is a short, explosive race in heats.
  5. Mandatory safety gear is required in longer races.

Lingo you'll hear

Skins

Transition
