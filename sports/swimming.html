Swimming

The Olympics' biggest medal haul: four strokes, many distances.

Individual and relays

The Olympics and the World Aquatics Championships

What is it?

Swimming is one of the headline sports of the Summer Olympics, with 35 events. Swimmers race in lanes using four strokes: freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke and butterfly.

Because there are so many events, one swimmer can win a huge medal haul. Michael Phelps won 8 golds at a single Games in 2008.

The basic rules

  1. There are four strokes. Individual medley (IM) races use all four.
  2. A false start means disqualification.
  3. Breaststroke and butterfly swimmers must touch the wall with both hands at once.
  4. Swimmers may stay underwater for only 15 meters after a start or turn.
  5. Relays have 4 swimmers, each swimming one leg.
