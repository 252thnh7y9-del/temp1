Badminton

The fastest racket sport on Earth. That's no backyard game.

Players
Singles, doubles or mixed doubles
Match length
Best of 3 games
Season
Year-round on the BWF World Tour
Biggest events
The Olympics, the World Championships and the All England Open

What is it?

Badminton is played with light rackets and a feathered shuttlecock over a high net. Professional smashes have been clocked at well over 400 km/h (250 mph), making it the fastest racket sport in the world.

It's a huge sport in Asia, especially Indonesia, China, Malaysia, India, Japan and South Korea, and in Denmark. Top players are national celebrities there.

The basic rules

  1. Games go to 21 points and must be won by 2, with a cap at 30.
  2. Every rally scores a point.
  3. The shuttle may never touch the ground on your side. You get one hit to return it.
  4. Serves are hit underhand and diagonally into the opponent's service box.
  5. Touching the net with your body or racket loses the point.

Major leagues & competitions

  • Olympic GamesFive events: men's and women's singles and doubles, plus mixed doubles.
  • BWF World ChampionshipsThe yearly world title.
  • All England OpenThe oldest and most prestigious tournament, held in Birmingham.
  • Thomas Cup / Uber CupMen's and women's world team championships.

Powerhouse nations

  • ChinaThe most successful nation overall.
  • IndonesiaBadminton is a national obsession there.
  • DenmarkEurope's powerhouse.
  • Japan / South Korea / IndiaStrong modern contenders.

Famous names to know

  • Lin DanChinese, two Olympic golds. Often called the greatest.
  • Lee Chong WeiMalaysian icon, world No. 1 for years. Lin Dan's great rival.
  • Viktor AxelsenDanish two-time Olympic champion.
  • Carolina MarínSpanish Olympic champion who broke Asia's dominance.
  • PV SindhuIndia's two-time Olympic medalist.
  • Taufik HidayatIndonesian 2004 Olympic champion.

Lingo you'll hear

Shuttlecock / birdie
The feathered projectile.
Smash
A powerful downward hit.
Clear
A high, deep shot to the back of the court.
Drop shot
A soft shot that falls just over the net.

💬 Sound smart at a party

“Footwork is everything.” Elite players cover the court in just a few steps.
