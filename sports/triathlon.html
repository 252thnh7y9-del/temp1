Triathlon

Swim, bike, run, back to back. First across the line wins.

Competitors
Individual and mixed relay
Olympic distance
1.5 km swim, 40 km bike, 10 km run
Time
About 1 hour 45 minutes for elites
Biggest events
The Olympics and the Ironman World Championship

What is it?

Triathletes complete three endurance sports in a row: swimming, cycling and running. The clock keeps running during the transitions, when athletes switch gear.

The Olympic distance takes elite athletes about 1 hour 45 minutes. The longer Ironman races, which aren't at the Olympics, take most of a day.

The basic rules

  1. Everyone starts together. The first across the finish line wins.
  2. Transitions (T1 swim-to-bike, T2 bike-to-run) count toward your time.
  3. Drafting on the bike is allowed in Olympic races, but not in most Ironman races.
  4. Penalties for rule breaks are served in a penalty box.
  5. The mixed relay has four athletes each doing a short triathlon.
